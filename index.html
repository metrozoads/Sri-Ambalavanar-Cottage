Welcome

A home for pilgrims, on the temple's own street.

Sri Ambalavanar Cottage opened on 3 March 2025 at 17, Mayuranathar Sannathi Street. Pilgrims, families and travellers come here for the same reasons: spotless rooms, a calm stay, and the temple just a short walk away.

We have 20 rooms that sleep up to 40 guests, a 24-hour reception, CCTV across the premises and fresh linen and towels in every room.

அம்பலவாணர்

Ambalavanar is Lord Nataraja, the one who dances in the golden hall (ambalam). His dancing form, inside its ring of flames, is the emblem of our cottage.

Temples nearby

Your base for the temples of the Cauvery delta.

Mayiladuthurai sits at the centre of the Navagraha temple circuit. Start your day at Mayuranathar and reach the others by car.
