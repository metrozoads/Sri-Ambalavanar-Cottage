    Questions
    
      
    What guests usually ask.
    
      
    Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll answer quickly.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    What are the check-in and check-out times?
    You can check in and check out any time between 12:00 AM and 11:00 PM. Just let us know your arrival time in advance so someone is ready to receive you.
    
      
    Do you serve breakfast?
    Breakfast isn't included in the room rate, but you can add a home-style South Indian breakfast while booking. Several messes and tiffin centres are also about 900 m away.
    
      
    Is there pickup from the railway station or airport?
    Yes, as an add-on. Mayiladuthurai Junction is 3.5 km away, and we can also arrange a car from Puducherry Airport (109 km).
    
      
    Can I park my car?
    Yes. Free private parking is available on site.
    
      
    Is there a restaurant or swimming pool?
    There's no restaurant or pool at the cottage. S V R Mess, Muniyandi Villas Tiffin Centre and Nalini Cafe are all about 900 m away.
    
      
    Is it good for families with children?
    Children of all ages are welcome. Children 11 and above are charged as adults, and cribs or extra beds aren't available.
